    My approach

    Automation should solve a problem, not create another one.

    I believe good automation starts with understanding how a business actually works. The right solution should fit the process, the people using it, and the tools already in place.

    1. ?

      Understand

      Identify the business problem and understand the existing workflow.

    2. Evaluate

      Determine whether automation is appropriate and identify manual work or system gaps.

    3. Design

      Map out a practical workflow that addresses the identified problem.

    4. Build and Test

      Develop and test the workflow before considering it ready for actual use.

    5. Refine

      Review how the workflow performs and identify opportunities for improvement.
